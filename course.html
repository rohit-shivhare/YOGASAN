Vrikshasana
(Tree Pose) :

Benefits of Vrikshasana

1. Improves Balance and Coordination: Vrikshasana helps to improve balance and coordination by strengthening the muscles in the legs, ankles, and feet.

2. Strengthens the Ankles and Calves: This pose helps to strengthen the muscles in the ankles and calves.

3. Improves Posture: Vrikshasana helps to improve posture by strengthening the muscles in the back and core.

4. Reduces Stress and Anxiety: This pose can help to reduce stress and anxiety by promoting relaxation and calmness.

5. Improves Focus and Concentration: Vrikshasana helps to improve focus and concentration by training the mind to stay present and focused.
